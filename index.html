What Do You Know About Endangered Animals?

In recent years, human activities such as deforestation and industrailisation has caused many animal species to become extinct.

To reduce the effects of human activities on wildlife and ecosystems, governments and nonprofit organisations around the world have started to take action.

In this page, you will learn about a variety of endangered animals and the steps taken to protect them.
