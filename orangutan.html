IUCN status: Critically Endangered

Orangutan

Pongo

Orangutans are highly intelligent great apes found only on the islands of Borneo and Sumatra. As fruit-eaters and seed dispersers, they help rainforest plants spread and regenerate. Their populations are declining because deforestation, fires, palm-oil development and other land conversion destroy the forest habitat on which they depend.

Recent population statistics

About 104,700 Bornean orangutans estimated in the wild
About 13,846 Sumatran orangutans estimated in the wild
Fewer than 800 Tapanuli orangutans estimated in the wild

Latest widely published estimates: WWF species data and IUCN assessments, updated through 2023.
